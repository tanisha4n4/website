about.

fournfour is a multidisciplinary studio working across identity, digital experiences, objects, spaces and visual culture. We move between disciplines rather than staying within one, following ideas wherever they need to go. A visual identity might become a website, an object might become a space, and a spatial idea might begin with an image, a conversation or something found by accident. Our practice is rooted in curiosity, research and experimentation. We think through making, and make through thinking collecting references, testing ideas and allowing the process to shape the outcome. Each project becomes its own small world: considered, curious and built from the connections between things that might not initially seem related.
