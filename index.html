FOUR N FOUR is an independent creative practice by two friends who have spent the last eight years sharing ideas, references, ambitions and increasingly questionable career decisions.

We come from different places, with different ways of looking at things, but somewhere along the way we found ourselves returning to the same instinct: make it, test it, pull it apart, look at it again, then make it better.

Our work moves between branding, graphic design, digital experiences, objects, products, furniture, campaigns, installations and spatial ideas.

We are interested in the thinking as much as the final form, and in the small details that make something feel considered.

FOUR N FOUR is built as an open practice, a place for experimentation, collaboration and making.

We work with ideas and make them real in whatever form makes sense.

ABOUT.

WE MAKE
THINGS.

FOUR N FOUR is an independent creative practice by two friends who have spent the last eight years sharing ideas, references, ambitions and increasingly questionable career decisions.

We come from different places, with different ways of looking at things, but somewhere along the way we found ourselves returning to the same instinct: make it, test it, pull it apart, look at it again, then make it better.

Our work moves between branding, graphic design, digital experiences, objects, products, furniture, campaigns, installations and spatial ideas. We are interested in the thinking as much as the final form, and in the small details that make something feel considered.

FOUR N FOUR is built as an open practice, a place for experimentation, collaboration and making.

We work with ideas and make them real in whatever form makes sense.

Sometimes that means a visual identity. Sometimes it is a website, an object, a package, a chair, a campaign, a space or something else entirely.

We make things.
